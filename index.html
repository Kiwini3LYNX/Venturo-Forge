Shape your system.

Welcome to your forge!

Build your workshop. Your workers carry materials and operate the stations. Short routes help, but every investment needs to pay off.

How a hammer is made:
⛓️ Iron storage→🔥 Forge→⚒️ Anvil
🪵 Wood storage→🔨 Assembly→📦 Shipping

Two separate material flows meet at Assembly: the forged hammerhead from the anvil and the wooden handle from storage.

  • Place all six stations and choose your workers’ starting positions. The production sequence is fixed; the layout is yours.
  • Each round runs for 120 simulation seconds. Workers must physically carry every item.
  • Adjust staffing, upgrade stations, or buy additional iron/wood stores. Your cash is limited; unspent cash carries forward, and purchases are permanent.
  • Only sold hammers earn revenue. Market demand may change!

There are three rounds. Future demand is revealed one round at a time. Your goal is to build a profitable workshop across all three.
